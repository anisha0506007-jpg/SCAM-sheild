      LEARN & PROTECT

      Your online safety guide

      Simple habits can help you avoid common online scams.

      🔗

      Check links carefully

      Look closely at the website address. Avoid unexpected links and misspelled domains.

      LINK SAFETY
      🔑

      Never share OTPs

      Never share an OTP, PIN, or password with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

      ACCOUNT SAFETY
      ⏱

      Pause under pressure

      Scammers may create urgency. Verify the request through a trusted contact.

      SOCIAL ENGINEERING
      💳

      Verify payments

      Be cautious of unexpected fees, guaranteed returns, prize claims, and money transfers.

      PAYMENT SAFETY
      💼

      Check job offers

      Research the employer on its official website. Be wary of paying fees to secure a job.

      JOB SCAMS
      📞

      Verify callers

      Contact the claimed organization using an independently verified number.
